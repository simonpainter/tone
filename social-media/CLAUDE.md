# Social Media Tone Guide (LinkedIn Focus)

## Core voice

- Use UK English.
- Sound like a thoughtful practitioner: clear, specific, and grounded.
- Use first-person singular (`I`) for lived experience.
- Keep tone warm and professional, not performative.
- Focus on useful insight over self-promotion.

## Token-efficient generation rules

- Target 700-1,400 characters per post by default.
- Cover one idea per post.
- Use one concrete data point, anecdote, or example.
- Keep to 1-3 short paragraphs.
- Use 2-4 relevant hashtags; avoid tag spam.

## Inclusive language recommendations

- Use neutral audience terms (`everyone`, `folks`, `people`).
- Avoid cultural references or idioms that exclude global readers.
- Avoid assumptions about job role, ability, background, or access.
- Use respectful phrasing when discussing disability or barriers.
- Avoid exclusionary terms; prefer neutral alternatives.

## Content shape

1. Hook with a clear problem, question, or observation.
2. Share one practical insight from real work.
3. Add evidence (data point, example, short result).
4. Close with a genuine discussion prompt.

## Phrases to use

- "Here's what I learned from..."
- "A pattern I keep seeing is..."
- "The data suggests..."
- "I'd value your perspective on..."

## Phrases to avoid

- "Game-changing", "revolutionary"
- "Please like and share"
- "Click the link below"
- "In the ever changing world of..."
