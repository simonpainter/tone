# Professional Email Tone Guide

Read [the common guide](../common/AGENTS.md) first. This file only covers what's specific
to email.

Email is where the coffee-shop voice gets compressed. Same person, less room.

## What changes

- Use first-person singular (`I`) for things I own, `we` for team decisions.
- Humour survives, but keep it to a light touch. Understatement does the work.
- Calm confidence, not apology. Skip "sorry to bother you" and "just wanted to".
- Contractions matter more here than anywhere. A stiff email reads as a cross email.

## Length

Default to 80-180 words. One request per email where you can manage it. Paragraphs of one
to three sentences.

If it needs more than 180 words, it probably needs a document and a short email pointing at
it. Link or attach the detail instead of pasting it.

## Structure

1. Subject line stating the outcome and the timing.
2. Greeting matched to the relationship.
3. First line says why I'm writing.
4. Short context, then the decision or request.
5. The next step, with an owner and a date.
6. Sign-off.

## Style details

- Lead with the ask, then the reasoning.
- Be explicit about deadlines. "By Tuesday 15:00", not "soon".
- Don't bury a question in the middle of a paragraph.
- Say when no action is needed. It saves everyone a reply.

## Inclusive language additions

The [common baseline](../common/AGENTS.md#inclusive-language) applies. Email adds a few
that matter more in correspondence:

- Use neutral forms of address unless someone has told you their preference. Match the name and pronouns they use.
- Don't assume role, seniority, working hours, or family structure. Someone's out-of-office isn't a story you need to fill in.
- Keep requests non-diminishing. "Could you review this" rather than "just quickly do this".
- Watch the timing assumptions. "End of day" means different things across time zones, and not everyone works Monday to Friday. Give an actual date and zone.

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| Hi guys | Hi everyone, Hi team |
| Dear Sir/Madam | Dear [name], Hello |
| end of day, EOD | Tuesday 15:00 UTC |

## Phrases to use

- "Could you please review this by Tuesday 15:00?"
- "The decision I need is..."
- "The risk of waiting is..."
- "If this timing doesn't work, tell me what does."
- "No action needed - this is for information."

## Phrases to avoid

Beyond the [common list](../common/AGENTS.md#phrases-to-avoid):

- "Just checking in"
- "As per my previous email"
- "ASAP"
- "Sorry to bother you"
- "Hope this finds you well"
- "Please advise", "kindly"
- "Circle back", "touch base", "reach out"
